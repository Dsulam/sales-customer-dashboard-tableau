# Tableau Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

**Interactive Sales Performance & Customer Insights Dashboards**  
*Year-over-Year Analysis for 2021–2023 | United States Retail Data*

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This Tableau project delivers **two professional, interactive dashboards** designed to help sales managers, executives, and analysts monitor business performance:

- **Sales Dashboard** — Provides a comprehensive view of sales metrics, trends, profitability, and product subcategory performance with year-over-year comparisons.
- **Customer Dashboard** — Focuses on customer acquisition, behavior, loyalty, and top-performing customers.

The dashboards enable users to **dynamically switch between years** (2021, 2022, 2023) and instantly compare performance against the previous year using filters and visual indicators.

**Data Scope**: United States retail transaction data covering multiple years, allowing flexible year-over-year analysis.

---

## 🎯 Dashboards & Key Requirements Met

### 1. Sales Dashboard

**Purpose**: Deliver an overview of sales performance and trends to analyze year-over-year results and understand sales patterns.

**Features Implemented**:

- **KPI Overview Cards**
  - Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Quantity
  - Year-over-Year (YoY) percentage change with visual indicators (▲ / ▼)
  - Comparison between selected year and previous year

- **Sales Trends (Monthly)**
  - Line charts showing monthly performance for the selected year vs. previous year
  - Clear highlighting of **Highest Month** and **Lowest Month** for easy identification

- **Product Sub-Category Comparison**
  - Side-by-side comparison of sales performance across sub-categories for current vs. previous year
  - Profit/Loss breakdown by sub-category
  - Categories covered: **Furniture** (Bookcases, Chairs, Furnishings, Tables), **Office Supplies** (Appliances, Art, Binders, Envelopes, Fasteners, Labels, Paper, Storage, Supplies), **Technology** (Accessories, Copiers, Machines, Phones)

- **Weekly Sales & Profit Trends**
  - Detailed weekly view of sales and profit
  - Average weekly reference lines
  - Visual highlighting of weeks performing **Above** and **Below** average

### 2. Customer Dashboard

**Purpose**: Provide insights into customer metrics, trends, and behavior to help marketing and management teams understand customer segments and improve satisfaction.

**Features Implemented**:

- **KPI Overview Cards**
  - Total Customers, Total Orders, and Average Sales per Customer
  - Year-over-Year percentage changes with visual indicators

- **Customer Trends (Monthly)**
  - Monthly trends for customer count, orders, and sales per customer
  - Comparison against previous year

- **Customer Distribution by Number of Orders**
  - Histogram/bar chart showing how customers are distributed based on the number of orders they have placed
  - Insights into customer loyalty and engagement levels

- **Top 10 Customers by Profit**
  - Ranked table of top customers
  - Includes: Rank, Customer Name, Most Recent Order Date, Profit, Sales, and Number of Orders
  - Helps identify high-value customers for targeted strategies

## 🚀 How to Use the Dashboard

1. Open the Tableau workbook (`.twbx` file).
2. Navigate between the **Sales Dashboard** and **Customer Dashboard** tabs.
3. Use the **Year filter** (top of dashboard) to select 2021, 2022, or 2023.
4. The dashboards automatically update to show:
   - Performance for the selected year
   - Comparison against the previous year
5. Apply additional filters (Region, Segment, Category, etc.) as needed.
6. Hover over charts for detailed tooltips and click elements for cross-highlighting.
